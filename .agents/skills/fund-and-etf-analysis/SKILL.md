# Fund And Etf Analysis

Identify exact fund/share class/ISIN, domicile, listing, trading/base currency,
benchmark, distribution policy, and hedging. Different exchange tickers may be
one share class; trading currency alone does not define underlying FX exposure.

- Read current issuer documents, fee schedules, holdings, and index methodology.
  Record document and holdings dates separately.
- Examine replication, sampling, derivatives, securities lending, collateral,
  leverage/inverse resets, and index rebalancing as relevant. Daily leverage does
  not promise the same multiple over longer holding periods.
- Compare ongoing charges, tracking difference, spreads, commissions, FX, and
  relevant tax leakage. Keep expense estimates separate from observed tracking
  difference; do not add fees twice when already reflected in returns/differences.
- Match periods, currency, distributions, total-return and gross/net benchmark
  definitions. Tracking error is variability of differences, not their average.
- If a supplied tracking difference lacks benchmark, period, annualization, or
  sign definition, retain it only as a supplied value with the missing definition
  visible. Withhold underperformance interpretation, annualization, currency
  equivalents, and complete-cost ranking that depend on those missing facts.
- Look through holdings for issuer/sector/country/factor/currency overlap. Quantify
  only disclosed coverage; partial/stale holdings leave residual exposure unknown.
  Fund count alone does not establish diversification.
- Assess spreads, underlying-market liquidity, premium/discount to NAV, size, and
  closure risk. Trading volume alone is not full liquidity evidence. Match quote
  and NAV timestamps before comparing them.
- Verify jurisdiction-specific withholding, domicile, account, and distribution/
  accumulation effects before after-tax recommendations. Listing venue alone
  does not determine tax treatment.

Return dated comparisons, exposure/overlap, cost definitions, structural differences,
suitability conditions, and unknowns. Apply `$investment-portfolio-analysis` for
personal recommendations; software analytics need no personal suitability interview.
