# Market Announcements And Earnings

Resolve issuer, listing/exchange, event, publication time/timezone, and requested
price window. Read the original disclosure before summarizing headlines.

- Prefer regulatory filings, exchange disclosures, company investor relations,
  official releases, and attributable transcripts. Distinguish annual/quarterly
  reports, material-event disclosures, and foreign-issuer forms. For Israeli
  issuers consult MAYA/MAGNA disclosures as available.
- Separate event date, first public disclosure, article update, effective date,
  and exchange session. Deduplicate syndicated/corrected stories. An after-close
  announcement cannot explain an earlier session's move.
- Classify earnings, guidance, financing, M&A, contracts, litigation, management,
  governance, regulation, dividends, splits, and other actions. Separate announced,
  agreed, approved, completed, and cancelled states.
- Compare earnings with comparable prior periods and prior management guidance.
  Use consensus only if a dated pre-release source exists. Keep GAAP/IFRS,
  adjusted, quarterly/annual, and per-share/total figures distinct; inspect
  reconciliations and share-count changes.
- Reconcile releases with statements/notes when available. Flag missing filings,
  audit/restatement issues, nonrecurring gains, working-capital changes, and
  cash-flow contradictions instead of trusting headline EPS.
- Identify what is new and its revenue, margin, cash, debt, dilution, or valuation
  channel. Attribute management claims and unverified rumors; sentiment is not
  a confirmed financial fact.
- Verify moves using dated prices, currency, session, and adjustment basis.
  Separate benchmark-relative and raw moves. Association alone is not causation;
  examine simultaneous company and macro events.
- For monitoring requests, identify feed coverage/delay, scheduling, access,
  failure, and retry behavior. On-demand research is not a running alert service
  and a web headline is not a verified live feed.

Return what happened, what changed, evidence/timestamps, transmission, alternatives,
unknowns, and follow-up filings. Use report/valuation skills for deeper analysis
and the event-impact workflow for measured scenarios.
