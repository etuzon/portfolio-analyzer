# Equity Valuation And Financial Modeling

Define valuation date, listing/price, currency, share basis, business model, and
purpose. A financial quality score is not fair value or expected stock return.

- Connect revenue drivers, margins, taxes, working capital, capex, depreciation,
  debt, interest, cash, and diluted shares. Check statement consistency; do not
  forecast income without its funding requirements.
- Separate reports, normalized adjustments, guidance, sourced consensus, and
  analyst assumptions. Do not invent unavailable forecasts.
- Distinguish enterprise/equity value. Document cash/debt, leases, minority
  interests, nonoperating assets, and other claims in the bridge; avoid counting
  items already included in cash flow or enterprise value twice.
- Discount FCFF using a consistent WACC and FCFE using an equity cost. Match
  currency, nominal/real basis, inflation, and cash-flow timing. Label assumed
  discount/debt rates separately from measured inputs.
- For perpetuities require a defensible steady state and discount rate above
  growth. Show terminal-value dependence and sensitivities to growth, margins,
  reinvestment, and discount rate. Do not clamp an invalid denominator.
- Match multiple numerator/denominator definitions and periods. Negative or tiny
  earnings can invalidate P/E; sector measures need explicit definitions. A peer
  median is context, not automatic value.
- Account for dilution, stock compensation, convertibles, buybacks, and ADR/share
  ratios without deducting the same economic cost twice.
- Use sector-appropriate models for banks, insurers, REITs, or resource businesses;
  read the principal analyst's specialist guidance when relevant.
- Reverse valuation asks which growth/margin/capital assumptions a price implies.
  Compare with business evidence; price is not proof that assumptions will occur.

Return assumptions, reconciliations, valuation range, price comparison,
sensitivities, counter-case, and invalidation conditions. State horizon and
dividend/dilution assumptions for return scenarios. Scenario weights are not
calibrated probabilities without supporting evidence. Use the spreadsheet skill
if producing a spreadsheet artifact.
