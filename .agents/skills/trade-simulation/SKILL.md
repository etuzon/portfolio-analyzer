# Trade Simulation

Use the existing dashboard and account ledger as the evidence for a proposed trade basket. Inspect `web/trade-simulator.js`, `web/trade-simulator-ui.js`, the context endpoint in `app/main.py`, and `riskScoreForItems` in `web/app.js` before changing behavior.

## Financial contract

- Simulation is read-only: never call transaction-save endpoints, alter the source ledger, or submit broker orders. Drafts belong to the current authenticated browser session and clear on sign-out/user change.
- Pressing Simulate captures a private snapshot for that request. Check local version consistency while capturing accounts, transactions and holdings; later account/market changes must not cancel an already coherent snapshot during listing lookup or erase its result. Copy mutable source records before lookup. Deduplicate listing requests without collapsing account positions. Reject superseded browser responses on basket edits, a newer explicit calculation, and sign-out, rather than on live portfolio refreshes.
- Apply rows in order to shared projected cash and quantities per account. A sale cannot exceed the projected long holding or silently open a short. A short cannot coexist with a long in the same account/listing. Buy covers a short first and can cross zero into a long.
- Treat listed RSU shares in Investment accounts as supported shares, using their existing listing identity and the shared direct-equity scenarios. RSU classification alone does not make a holding a derivative or an unvested grant. Preserve the separate rejection of actual option/future subclasses and ESOP trading accounts. Check real imported asset classifications when reproducing unsupported-instrument errors; synthetic EQUITY-only fixtures miss this boundary. Explain unsupported asset classes separately from derivatives.
- Cash changes by `−quantity × execution price − fee` for buys and `+quantity × execution price − fee` for sells/shorts. Fees are explicit, in account currency. Do not invent taxes, borrow costs, interest, automatic currency conversion, or broker eligibility.
- Normalize entered prices using instrument conventions: Israeli prices in agorot and US bond prices in cents divide by 100; ordinary US stock prices are dollars. Saved quotes are already normalized. Keep execution price distinct from the saved valuation quote, and show its actual quote date. Blank execution price uses a dated saved quote; an explicit hypothetical price is identified as user input.
- Net account value is signed securities value plus ledger cash. Gross exposure sums absolute securities values. Show short exposure separately. Short proceeds do not create account equity or establish spendable cash. Available cash remains unknown for shorts or derivatives unless a user-specified, clearly described collateral model is implemented; never imply a model is a verified broker rule.
- Reuse the dashboard's shared `portfolio-risk-v3` scenario-loss severity model and its `portfolio-risk-provisional-v2` asset-aware estimates. Read [the implemented risk formula](references/portfolio-risk.md) before cash/debt or score changes. Score is `round(min(100, 200 * largest modeled loss / positive net equity))` from pooled signed exposures and ledger cash. Borrowing reduces equity; sum account borrowing separately even when combined cash is positive. There is no component-point borrowing multiplier or 75/25 account-score blend. Known nonpositive equity with securities/debt scores100. This is declared scenario severity, not loss probability, VaR, an exhaustive maximum loss or broker margin permission.
- Preserve unknown cash, missing price/date/classification, unknown account attribution, and absent FX; do not coerce them to zero or silently omit them from totals. The strict score stays incomplete. Eligible last-known values and asset proxies may support a labelled automatic estimate; unresolved monetary or payoff scope displays a range, with known-position results secondary. Keep known account results visible when another account is incomplete. Show specific per-metric reasons, affected security symbols, and the Investment accounts blocking total risk. Distinguish unknown broker collateral from missing ledger cash. Stale quotes retain their actual dates and a visible warning.
- Planner financial projections follow the same ledger equation as the simulator: buys subtract notional and fees; shorts add proceeds and subtract fees. Reserved collateral affects the planner's spending policy, not net equity or financial-risk cash. Both actions preserve net equity before fees. Mutate projected positions only on exact symbol, normalized market, currency and asset-class identity; preserve other listings separately. Issuer aggregation for shared risk and concentration limits is a separate step.
- The current position snapshot includes all ledger dates. Reject simulation in an account containing future-dated security trades and mark its valuation/risk unavailable, while retaining known current cash. Excluding future cash movements alone is insufficient: future purchases can otherwise create fictitious holdings and future full sales can hide existing ones.
- Date-only ledger entries use the Asia/Jerusalem calendar day derived from the captured timestamp. Include that day's cash and security activity even before UTC midnight; exclude later cash and reject later security activity. The planner retains its conservative reservation of future debits. Keep quote freshness on the captured instant, and never substitute the live clock for a frozen snapshot. Verify winter/summer midnight and DST boundaries.
- Include only Investment accounts in simulation account cards and combined cash, value, exposure and risk totals, including unchanged Investment accounts. Exclude Bank, Long-Term Savings, pension and ESOP accounts and their holdings, warnings and missing-data penalties. Keep orphan holdings honestly unavailable unless an excluded owner/type is established. Reuse saved listing/price evidence from excluded accounts for a requested security when needed, without counting their positions or cash. Label totals as Investment accounts; they are not total household wealth.

## Interaction and verification

Keep a dedicated Trade Simulation entry within the existing hierarchical navigation. Let the user add, duplicate, edit, and remove rows across accounts, then calculate the whole basket. Keep numerical fields readable and preserve drafts on error/reopen/language change. Only scenario edits mark its result stale. Display the capture time and explain that results stay frozen while live accounts/analytics update; an explicit Simulate captures fresh data. Do not subscribe simulator validity to the dashboard's live JSON fingerprint or refresh cycle. Loading must stay inside the simulator.

Maintain the corresponding Help in `web/index.html` and Hebrew in `web/i18n.js`. Cover account prerequisites, price/fee units, row order, buy-to-cover, short uncertainty, total scope, risk limitations, saved evidence, and retry.

Verify deterministic raw-fixture calculations for repeated securities/accounts, partial/full sales, short/cover, fees, currencies, missing evidence, negative cash, overflow, and no input mutation. Verify stale-response rejection and sign-out privacy, then review desktop/mobile and both languages. Follow the project's engineering triad for independent QA of functional changes; simulation authorization does not authorize deployment.
