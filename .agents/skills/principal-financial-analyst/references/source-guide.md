# Financial Source Selection

Choose sources here, then verify specific documents at task time. These source
families are not installed connectors or guarantees of API coverage. Discover
available tools before asking the user to paste public information.

| Evidence | Preferred sources | Check |
|---|---|---|
| US filings | [EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/), [SEC APIs](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces), issuer IR | Issuer/listing, publication/amendment dates, periods, custom tags and segment coverage |
| Israeli disclosures | [MAYA](https://maya.tase.co.il/), [MAGNA](https://www.magna.isa.gov.il/), issuer IR | Original disclosure, issuer/security, publication/effective time, amendments |
| Policy and FX | [Bank of Israel](https://www.boi.org.il/en/), [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov/), [ECB](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/) | Decision versus implementation, observed versus forecast, rate and quote definitions |
| Economic releases | [Israel CBS](https://www.cbs.gov.il/en/), [BLS](https://www.bls.gov/), [BEA](https://www.bea.gov/), [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat) | Release time, period, seasonal/annualized basis, revisions |
| Historical macro knowledge | [ALFRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/docs/api/fred/alfred.html), archived official releases | Actual publication by cutoff and vintage coverage |
| Prices and actions | Official exchange/issuer and existing provider abstractions | Delay, session, timezone, adjustment basis, dividends, currency, licensing |
| Funds and bonds | Prospectus, factsheet, index methodology, offering documents | Exact share class/instrument, date, costs, cash-flow and quote conventions |
| Consensus and calls | Dated attributable estimates and official calls/transcripts | Pre-release availability, coverage, access, transcript accuracy; may require paid data |

Secondary reporting can reveal events and competing explanations; verify material
claims against originals where available. Attribute unverified claims and separate
social sentiment from disclosures. Retain sufficient metadata to reproduce input
availability, meaning, and calculations; retrieval time is not publication time.
Respect provider/cache contracts and keep credentials out of artifacts. If access
or coverage is missing, identify the exact gap and continue only conclusions not
dependent on it. Do not invent prices, consensus, subscriptions, or monitoring.
