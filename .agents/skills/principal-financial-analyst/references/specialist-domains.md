# Conditional Financial Specialties

Read relevant sections only. These modules are not separate agents or claims of
application instrument support.

## Sector Accounting

- Banks: examine capital, asset quality, provisions, net interest margin, funding,
  liquidity, and regulation. Industrial debt/revenue ratios and FCFF may not fit.
- Insurers: separate underwriting, reserves, investments, reinsurance, solvency,
  and product duration.
- REITs/property: reconcile FFO/AFFO, asset value, occupancy, leases, maintenance
  capex, maturities, and rate exposure; FFO/AFFO are not GAAP net income.
- Resources: production, reserves, costs, realized prices, depletion, sustaining
  capex, and cycle scenarios; spot price is not a permanent forecast.
- Show unsupported sectors as requiring a specialized model instead of forcing a
  generic score. Verify definitions and fiscal/currency comparability.

## Derivatives and Hedging

Resolve multiplier, underlying, strike, expiry, exercise/settlement, currency,
collateral, and market inputs. Separate expiry payoff/current value, notional/
capital at risk, and theoretical/executable prices. Model time, volatility, and
nonlinear shocks; disclose limits. Hedging depends on basis, tenor, size, costs,
and counterparties. Do not infer broker permission or expand app support.

## Market Structure and Technical Analysis

Check sessions, spreads, depth, volume, actions, auctions, halts, and settlement.
Separate descriptive indicators from tested forecasts. Declare signals and use
consistent prices; include costs, slippage, selection, and multiple testing before
claiming tradable value. Keep technical context separate from intrinsic value.

## Jurisdiction and Taxes

Establish residence, citizenship where relevant, account, fund domicile,
instrument, and date before personalized conclusions. Verify current official
authority/treaty documents. Separate withholding, final liability, and credits;
unknown context permits conditional comparisons, not definitive tax treatment.
Collect only necessary personal facts.

## Governance and Accounting Quality

Inspect auditor opinions, restatements, related parties, control, incentives,
dilution, capital allocation, and disclosed litigation. Reconcile earnings, cash,
and balance-sheet changes. Attribute supported red flags; do not allege fraud
from unexplained anomalies alone.

## Behavioral Finance and Research Challenge

State a credible counter-thesis, base rates, priced-in expectations where supported,
and observable invalidation conditions. Check anchoring, confirmation bias, loss
aversion, narrative selection, and recency. Weigh evidence rather than manufacturing
equal support for weak claims. Preserve the original thesis during later evaluation.
