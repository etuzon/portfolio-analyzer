Research date: 27/10/2026. Event: the Brazilian presidential runoff on
25/10/2026, recorded under `brazil-presidential-runoff-2026-10-25`.

confirms the election outcome. Political/fiscal repricing is an attributed
explanation in the existing BBD case; the percentage caused by each driver is
unidentified.

## Geography and use in the app

US listing location is distinct from operating exposure. BBD, ITUB, PBR, ABEV,
VALE and SUZ are ADRs; XP, STNE and NU are US-listed ordinary shares. The shared
Brazil shock can affect all through their business exposures. NU's disclosed
operations in Mexico and Colombia do not prove losses in those operations from
this event. Foreign competitor gains and supplier/customer spillovers remain
unquantified until supported by exposure and trade evidence.

For ADR comparisons, local and USD returns may have different signs. With a fixed
ADR ratio and aligned timestamps, `(1 + USD return) = (1 + local return) /
(1 + change in BRL per USD)`. Actual local and US closes need not be simultaneous.
No isolated FX contribution is calculated here.

The app's `get_event_impact_cases` returns each case and the shared exposure map.
Future research should check the actual event surprise, relevant exposure,
currency and funding changes, company news, hedges and comparable prior events.
This is one event with nine correlated stock observations selected retrospectively.
It provides neither nine independent trials nor a calibrated probability.
Event-attributable revenue/profit percentages and future price effects remain
unknown. No model has been trained or updated.
